My name is Gleb Arinkin. My journey in the heritage world is built on years of handling the real thing. I've spent a year at Clochard 9.2 vintage store and over two years at DenimHeads Prague, immersing myself in the weight of raw denim and the soul of archival workwear. I know the industry from the inside — from the iconic store floors to collaborations with fellow makers. My mission is to spotlight the authenticity that trend-focused fashion ignores.
